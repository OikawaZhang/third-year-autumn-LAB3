A. Message vs. tf

1. Convert an incoming geometry_msgs::Quaternion to an existing tf2::Quaternion:
   tf2::fromMsg(quat_msg, quat_tf);

2. Convert tf2::Quaternion to a newly declared geometry_msgs::Quaternion:
   geometry_msgs::Quaternion quat_msg = tf2::toMsg(quat_tf);

3. Access the scalar component:
   quat_tf.getW();

B. Conversion

1. Extract yaw from tf2::Quaternion:
   double yaw = tf2::getYaw(quat_t);

2. Convert geometry_msgs::Quaternion to an Eigen 3-by-3 rotation matrix:
   Eigen::Quaterniond q(quat_msg.w, quat_msg.x, quat_msg.y, quat_msg.z);
   Eigen::Matrix3d R = q.normalized().toRotationMatrix();

Note: Eigen's four-scalar constructor uses the order (w, x, y, z), while ROS
message fields are commonly displayed as (x, y, z, w).
